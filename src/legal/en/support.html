FAQ

How do I sync between my devices?

Anqui syncs through your private iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on all your devices, and in system Settings, iCloud, check that Anqui has iCloud access. Changes can take a moment to appear. In Anqui's Settings, iCloud section, you can see the sync status.

Where is my data?

On your device and, if sync is on, in your private iCloud database. The developer has access to neither. More in the privacy policy.

How do I import decks?

In the app, tap Import and choose a .tsv file (for example, a plain-text notes export) or an .apkg file. Importing happens on your device. With an .apkg you can turn on "Import progress" to bring your Anki review history; otherwise cards start as new. .tsv cards always start as new.

How do I get better Japanese voices?

Anqui reads with the voices installed in iOS. To download a higher-quality one: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, Japanese, then pick a voice marked Enhanced or Premium to download it. On Mac: System Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, System Voice, Manage Voices. Exact menu names may vary by system version.

How do I delete my data?

Inside the app you can delete notes and decks. Uninstalling Anqui removes the data from the device. If you use iCloud, synced data is managed in system Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.
