Anqui 暗記

Anqui is an iPhone, iPad and Mac app for studying Japanese with spaced-repetition flashcards. It schedules each card with the FSRS algorithm, shows furigana, reads Japanese sentences aloud with the system voice, has widgets and daily reminders, and imports decks from .apkg and .tsv files. Your data stays on your device and, if you turn it on, in your private iCloud: Anqui has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics.
