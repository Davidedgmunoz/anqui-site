Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Summary

The developer of Anqui does not collect any data about you. Anqui has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and includes no third-party SDKs that send information off your device.

What the app stores

The notes, decks, review history and settings you create in Anqui are stored on your device. This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and the developer cannot see it.

iCloud sync

If you turn on sync, Anqui uses CloudKit to copy your data to your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. That database is yours alone: the developer has no access to it. Apple's iCloud terms govern how that data is stored and handled. If iCloud is off for Anqui or you are not signed in, the app works the same, on the device only.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications: your own device schedules and shows them. They are optional, the app asks for permission only when you turn them on, and nothing is sent to any server. You can turn them off in Anqui or in system Settings.

Speech

Read-aloud uses Apple's speech synthesis, which runs on your device. Anqui does not record your voice or use the microphone.

Importing decks

When you import an .apkg or .tsv file, it is read and processed locally on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Widgets

The widgets show your pending cards, your streak and words you have studied, using data the app shares with them inside your device. Nothing leaves the device.

Third parties

Anqui uses no advertising, analytics or third-party crash reporting. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics or crash reports, but only if you allow it in your device settings (Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements). These reports do not identify individuals.

Children

Anqui is not specifically directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because the developer does not have your data, there is nothing to ask them to delete. You can delete notes and decks inside the app, or uninstall it to remove the data from the device. Data synced to iCloud can be managed from your device's iCloud settings.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, for example if analytics are ever added, this policy will be updated before the change reaches the app and the effective date above will be revised.
