Credits and licenses

Anqui uses the following third-party software and references.

  • FSRS: the spaced-repetition algorithm (FSRS-6) is implemented from the formulas published on the FSRS wiki. The py-fsrs, fsrs-rs and ts-fsrs implementations (open-spaced-repetition, MIT license) were used as a reference and for known answers in tests; their code is not included in the app.
  • ZIPFoundation (Thomas Zoechling, MIT license): reading .apkg files.
  • Zstandard (Meta, used under the BSD 3-Clause license): decompressing .apkg collections.

Anqui does not currently use data from JMdict, KANJIDIC or KanjiVG. If they are added, they will be credited here.

Anqui is an independent app and is not affiliated with any other flashcard program.
